THE TRIP

With this trip, we were given the opportunity to spend two weeks in Lens, doing two different projects with students from different countries and with the opportunity to discover the city and its surroundings during our free time.

Ultimately, this trip ended up being an experience I will never forget. From participating in two really fun and different projects, to making friends and discovering new places like Lens, Arras, Lille, Vimy Ridge, and even Paris (which I had visited once before), the trip was an amazing experience I would recommend to anyone. I also loved being able to meet new people, everyone was super nice and for the french students I got to spend a lot of time with, I hope that wasn’t the last time I saw them. Lastly, I’m also grateful for the people from Canada that I was with and made everyday so much fun, and for the trip which allowed the already existing friendships to just become stronger.

All in all, it was SO much fun and I would do it all again in a heartbeat so thank you to the Vanier foundation for helping in making the trip happen.
